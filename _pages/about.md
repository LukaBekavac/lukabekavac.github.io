I am a PhD Candidate in Computer Science at the [University of St. Gallen (HSG)](https://www.unisg.ch), where I am part of the [Interactions research group](https://ics.unisg.ch/chair-interactions-mayer/) led by Prof. Dr. Simon Mayer.

My research focuses on understanding and addressing the systemic risks posed by Very Large Online Platforms (VLOPs). I combine methods from computer science, technology law, and the social sciences to study how platforms' personalized recommender systems influence us, while developing tools to enhance transparency and accountability in their operation. My work covers recommender systems, platform data access, and algorithmic auditing, and I publish at venues such as ACM FAccT, ACM Web Science, and ACM TORS.
